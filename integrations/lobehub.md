# Use CheapAIModel with LobeHub (LobeChat)

[LobeHub](https://github.com/lobehub/lobehub) is an open-source AI chat framework.

## Setup in 3 steps

1. Open **Settings → Model Providers** and click **+** at the top right.
2. Fill in:
   - **Provider ID:** `cheapaimodel`
   - **Request format:** OpenAI
   - **Proxy URL:** `https://api.cheapaimodel.com/v1`
   - **API Key:** your CheapAIModel key
3. Click **Fetch model list**, enable the models you want, and pick one in the chat.
